## Cross-cutting intent patterns (all 3 months)

1. **Make-ahead / timeline** — “what can I make ahead for Thanksgiving?” style queries convert well with calendar graphics.
2. **Beginner how-to** — turkey, gravy, mithai syrup stages, cookie-press fails: sensory + thermometer cues.
3. **Leftover remix** — high urgency, short half-life; publish by mid-November.
4. **Kid / party boards** — Halloween & Christmas visual formats; deep teal platters as brand signature.
5. **Diet filters** — vegan mithai, gluten-free sides, for-two turkey breast reduce SERP sameness.
